## Analysis Framework

For each account, track:
- **Follower count**
- **Avg views per reel**
- **Top 3 most viewed reels** (topic, hook, length, format)
- **Posting frequency** (videos/day or /week)
- **Content style** (talking head, text overlay, b-roll, memes)
- **Engagement rate** (likes + comments / views)
- **Caption style** (length, CTA, hashtags count)
