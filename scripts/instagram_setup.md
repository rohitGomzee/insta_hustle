## 📈 GROWTH HACKS (Organic)

### Daily (15-20 min):
1. **Engage with competitors' audiences:**
   - Go to @stockmarket.infomania, @edumcstark, etc.
   - Like top comments on their latest reels
   - Reply thoughtfully (not spammy)
   
2. **Reply to EVERY comment on your posts:**
   - Even if just "🙏" or "Thanks!"
   - Builds community, boosts algorithm

3. **Use trending audio:**
   - Check Instagram Reels trending section
   - Use low-volume background music

4. **Post at optimal times:**
   - 8-9 AM (pre-market)
   - 1-2 PM (lunch break)
   - 7-9 PM (post-market, leisure time)

### Weekly:
1. **Analyze performance:**
   - Which videos got most views?
   - Which got most saves?
   - Double down on winners

2. **Engage with followers:**
   - Reply to DMs
   - Acknowledge loyal commenters
   - Build relationships

3. **Cross-post:**
   - YouTube Shorts (same videos)
   - Twitter/X (clips + threads)
   - LinkedIn (professional angle)

## ⚠️ DON'TS

- ❌ Don't buy followers (kills engagement)
- ❌ Don't use banned hashtags (#trading might be shadowbanned, use #TradingStrategy instead)
- ❌ Don't post inconsistently (algorithm hates it)
- ❌ Don't ignore comments (engagement = reach)
- ❌ Don't give specific stock tips (SEBI compliance)
- ❌ Don't promise returns ("guaranteed profit" = red flag)
